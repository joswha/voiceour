# Voiceour ASR Bet A — conference-speech source inventory

**Scout:** CorpusSourceScout  
**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Scope:** licence-clean, real-speaker technical-speech corpus for Voiceour (commercial product). Training and evaluation only; **no redistribution** of recordings.  
**Jargon target:** 173 canonical terms in `/Users/vlad/Desktop/voiceoour/bench/autoresearch/jargon.terms.json` (`canonical` field), five domains: cloud, security, langs, Apple platform, data/web.  
**Method:** primary-source licence pages quoted verbatim; schedule/API enumeration; HTTP `curl -sI -L` HEAD of three media URLs per admissible source (2026-09-03). No media downloads beyond HEAD (Content-Length only).  
**Rule:** NC or ND terms disqualify training use. SA is not auto-disqualifying but is a redistribution/model-weight edge case (see Risks).

> You are free to:  
> **Share** — copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format for any purpose, even commercially.  
> **Adapt** — remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, even commercially.

Speaker-side publication notice (devroom CFP, not the archive footer). Read 2026-09-03 from `https://lists.fosdem.org/pipermail/fosdem/2024q4/003603.html` (Open Research devroom CFP for FOSDEM 2025):

> Talks have to be in English and will be recorded and later on published under Creative Commons CC-BY licence on the FOSDEM video recordings archive https://video.fosdem.org/.

**Website vs recordings:** FOSDEM HTML pages carry `meta-DC.Rights: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/be/` (e.g. schedule pages). That is **website content (BY-SA)**, distinct from the video archive's **CC BY 2.0 BE**. Do not treat schedule HTML as the recording licence.

**Transcripts/subtitles:** FOSDEM 2025 FAQ (`https://video.fosdem.org/2025/FAQ.txt`, read 2026-09-03) states VTT files are Whisper-tiny machine transcripts, **not** a separately licensed human work:

> What's with the .vtt files?  
> These are subtitle files that were generated by OpenAI whisper (the "tiny" model).  
> We do not guarantee quality or accuracy.

Treat VTT as weak term labels only. Copyright in an uncreative ASR dump is thin; do not depend on them as gold transcripts.

**Speaker consent:** Speakers review/release via `https://review.video.fosdem.org/overview` (FAQ). Opt-out is operational (talk not published), not a separate NC licence. Published files on `video.fosdem.org` are the CC BY corpus.

**Verdict:** Recordings **permit derivative datasets and commercial use** (CC BY, no NC/ND). Attribution required. Voiceour training use is in-licence.

#### Enumeration

- Schedule XML (Pentabarf): `https://fosdem.org/{YEAR}/schedule/xml` (2026 live; prior years `https://archive.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/schedule/xml`). Also `/schedule/ical`, `/schedule/xcal`.
- 2026 XML (read 2026-09-03) includes conference metadata, tracks (containers, security, rust, go, python, kernel, llvm, identity-and-access-management, virtualization-and-cloud-infrastructure, ebpf, swift, …), persons with names/slugs, events with room/times.
- Room occupation map: `https://archive.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/schedule/roomtracks/`
- Direct media tree: `https://video.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/{room}/`

#### Media URL pattern

```
https://video.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/{roomSlug}/{eventSlug}.av1.webm   # smallest; AV1+Opus (preferred)
https://video.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/{roomSlug}/{eventSlug}.mp4        # huge H.264 live-stream mezzanine
https://video.fosdem.org/{YEAR}/{roomSlug}/{eventSlug}.vtt        # Whisper tiny (2025+)
```

`video.fosdem.org` returns HTTP 302 to a geo mirror (`ftp.fau.de`, `mirrors.dotsrc.org`, Belnet, AS35701, …). Follow redirects (`curl -sI -L`).

Room slugs are lowercase without dots: `UD2.218A` → `ud2218a`; `K.1.105` → `k1105`; `UB4.132` → `ub4132`.

- **Hours (relevant tracks):** FOSDEM 2025 advertised 1105 events / 1193 speakers / 79 tracks. A full-day devroom is ~8 h of talking; half-day ~4 h. - **Size (smallest audio-bearing format = av1.webm):** 161 h × 0.15 GB/h ≈ **24 GB**.

## Disqualified sources with reasons

### PyCon US (YouTube / pyvideo.org) — DISQUALIFIED (NC for recipients)

Read 2026-09-03 from `https://us.pycon.org/2025/speaking/recording/`:

> I, the Submitter, also hereby grant each person receiving the User Submission distributed by the PSF a non-exclusive license to access, use, display, and perform the User Submission in a **non-commercial, private context**.

The PSF itself gets a broad licence (reproduce, derivatives, YouTube). **Downstream recipients — including Voiceour — only get NC/private.** NC is disqualifying.

### EuroPython — DISQUALIFIED (NC)

EuroPython 2020 speaker release, read 2026-09-03 from `https://ep2020.europython.eu/events/speaker-release-agreement/`:

> The society will make the presentation available to the public using a Creative Commons Attribution Non-Commercial Share-Alike license (CC-BY-NC-SA ) accompanied by a copyright statement. You can find details on this license here: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/

**NC is disqualifying.** Later years were not shown to have dropped NC; do not mine without a new BY-or-better quote.

### linux.conf.au 2022 — DISQUALIFIED (NC)

Read 2026-09-03 from `https://linux.conf.au/programme/proposals/`:

> We plan to release recordings of every talk at the conference under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) licence.

**NC is disqualifying.** Other LCA years may differ; this cited year cannot be used.

### Apple WWDC sessions — DISQUALIFIED (all rights reserved; no commercial copy)

Apple Website Terms of Use, read 2026-09-03 from `https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/terms/site.html`:

> Except as expressly provided in these Terms of Use, no part of the Site and no Content may be copied, reproduced, republished, uploaded, posted, publicly displayed, encoded, translated, transmitted or distributed in any way (including "mirroring") to any other computer, server, Web site or other medium for publication or distribution or for any commercial enterprise, without Apple’s express prior written consent.

Also: personal/non-commercial informational use only for downloaded product materials; prohibition on robots/spiders to acquire Content. WWDC videos on `developer.apple.com` are Apple copyrighted content, not CC. **Disqualifying** for a commercial ASR training corpus.

### KubeCon / CNCF / Linux Foundation YouTube — DISQUALIFIED (no public CC grant found)

Recordings live on the CNCF YouTube channel. No primary-source speaker release granting CC BY (or MIT) to **recipients** was found on 2026-09-03 (LF event pages, CNCF branding guidelines, KubeCon FAQs). Default YouTube licence is **Standard YouTube Licence** (watch-only, not a training grant). **Absence of a recipient licence is not evidence of CC.** Do not mine.

### Black Hat — DISQUALIFIED (no CC; commercial conference copyright)

No Creative Commons or MIT grant located on Black Hat video pages or speaker materials on 2026-09-03. Treat as all-rights-reserved commercial content. **Disqualifying.**

### DEF CON talk videos — DISQUALIFIED (distribution ≠ licence)

`media.defcon.org` hosts torrents/downloads. No CC/MIT grant was found on DEF CON media/index pages on 2026-09-03. Public availability is not a commercial-derivative licence. **Disqualifying** until a per-talk or archive-wide grant is quoted.

### RustConf, EuroRust, GopherCon — DISQUALIFIED (no CC grant found)

Typically YouTube / conference platforms under standard (non-CC) terms. No primary-source BY/MIT recipient licence found 2026-09-03. **Disqualifying.**

### Spoken Wikipedia / Spoken Wikipedia Corpora — not first-tranche (SA + domain)

## Risks

### Licence edge cases

- **FOSDEM BY vs website BY-SA:** train on `video.fosdem.org` files, not on scraped HTML. Attribute “Talk title, Speaker, FOSDEM YEAR, CC BY 2.0 BE”.
- **FOSDEM speaker review:** a published file is the consent artefact. Do not scrape unreleased review.video.fosdem.org.
- **CCC per-talk variance:** never inherit congress default. Require the BY 4.0 URL in metadata. Drop talks with no licence line.
- **CCC translations:** extra audio tracks are derivative performances; use `language=eng` opus only.
- **DebConf pre-2006 and MiniDebConfs:** MiniDebConfs sit in the same tree; CFP language is DebConf-specific. Prefer labelled `DebConf{YY}` folders. Pre-DC6 individual licences.
- **CC BY-SA (SWC, People's Speech SA split):** commercial OK, but SA may be argued to attach to a **distributed** model or dataset. Out of tranche.
- **Whisper VTT:** machine output; do not treat as a second copyrighted transcript corpus; OK as weak labels.

### Speaker consent norms

Conference publication under CC BY / MIT after speaker review/opt-out is the consent model for FOSDEM/DebConf/CCC-BY talks. That is **licence consent to the recording**, not a publicity-rights waiver. CC deeds themselves warn:

> other rights such as publicity, privacy, or moral rights may limit how you use the material.

Training that never publishes speaker audio or identifiable clips is the low-risk posture (already the product plan). Do not ship demo reels of named speakers without a separate release.

### PII

Talks may contain emails, Slack handles, production hostnames, customer names on slides (visually, not audio), live demos of secrets. Audio-only training still captures spoken emails/tokens. Keep a drop-list for talks whose titles/abstracts say “live incident”, “customer”, “passwords”. Do not train on Q&A hallway mics if any exist (FOSDEM official files are the talk recording).

### Redistribution limits

Voiceour **redistributes nothing**. Still:

- CC BY requires attribution **if** you share the corpus or a substantial excerpt internally in a way that is a “share”. Keep an attribution ledger (`title, speaker, source URL, licence URL`) even for training-only copies.
- Do not re-host mp4/webm/opus on a public bucket.
- Do not publish derived clips on YouTube.
- Model weights: BY (FOSDEM/CCC) does not copyleft weights. MIT (DebConf) does not copyleft weights. SA sources (excluded) might.

### Operational

- Mirror 302s: always `-L`; cache the final URL; be polite (one connection, Range probes only if needed, < 5 MB).
- FOSDEM av1.webm is the right object; mp4 is ~10× larger for similar audio.
- Apple-platform jargon will remain under-represented without WWDC. Do not “fix” that by mining Apple. Use FOSDEM Swift + later in-house recordings.

### Blocking issues

None for the three admissible sources. Legal review of CC BY 2.0 BE vs 4.0 (Belgium port) is optional, not blocking: both deeds grant commercial adaptation. CCC filter logic is a pipeline requirement, not a blocker.
