## What Wave 0 settled

- Streaming pre-encoding on the v3 weights is killed by inspection: the frontend z-scores each mel bin over the whole utterance and centre-pads the full sample array, and the `spk_ff1_w` load-time predicate gates that normalization together with the causal depthwise roll, so no configuration of the pinned weights has an incrementally equivalent prefix state. The stateful-encoder inventory corroborates it: no encoder cache in the C API, no stateful CoreML artifact.
- The real-speaker term corpus cannot be assembled from public rows; it is contributor-recorded under a consent instrument or it does not exist. Every Wave 3 fresh validation that needs real speech waits on that.
- Word-level confidence is already near-calibrated (ECE .018); the cited .621/.568 is a row-level artifact. Calibration work moves to term spans and stratified thresholds.
- The verifier and the conditioned decoder both have zero-edit seams in the shipped sidecar and the vendored decoder; their Wave 1 pilots run over cached evidence.
- Distillation and layerwise quantization are blocked on tooling and admissible sensitivity evidence respectively; the replacement screen is blocked on a frozen 48-row manifest whose R16 slice is derivable from the frontier verification run's per-row output.
