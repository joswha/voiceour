## Sealed cross-SoC acceptance rule (do not retune)

On any second Apple Silicon machine (different SoC family), at the promotion
candidate commit:

1. `scripts/vendor_parakeet.sh --check` green; `make test` green.
2. Accuracy/determinism: one native pass and two hybrid passes over the pinned
   general96 + jargon456 fixtures. REQUIRE uwer_mix(hybrid) ≤ .037509,
   jargon_false_terms = 0, and hybrid pass 1 byte-identical to pass 2.
3. Latency: general96 inference p95 ≤ 230 ms and RTFx ≥ 100 under the hybrid.
4. Energy: one full ABBA harness run under the established hygiene (idle-probe
   first; R-family ANE < 1 J; `df` headroom). REQUIRE energy_ratio ≤ 0.55 — the
   hybrid must keep at least half of the M4 Pro's measured saving, allowing for
   ANE generational variance.
5. Warm: `VOICEOUR_COREML_WARM=tiers` loads 3/3 configured tiers (log check).

Any failed endpoint blocks promotion and opens a diagnosis lane with its own
preregistration; the thresholds above do not move after the fact.
