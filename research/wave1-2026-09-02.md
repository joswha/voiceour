## What Wave 1 settled

- **Route A is closed on the v3 weights.** Neither greedy-lattice evidence (verifier) nor span-level confidence (calibration) can lower the shipped `.95` phonetic threshold at zero false activations: the failure is tail overlap on ordinary real speech, not lack of acoustic resolution. The shipped strict repair at 246/346 sits within +13 of the hindsight ceiling on this evidence.
- **Cached pair mining cannot supply contrastive negatives.** The surfaces the baseline emits for misses almost never occur as ordinary language in the existing corpora; pairs need the real-speaker corpus or a purpose-built ordinary-language set.
- **Teacher-disagreement mining yields 65 auditable distillation targets** with zero unsafe consensus, short of the 100 the data plan wanted; the balance depends on the real-speaker corpus.
- **The replacement-screen manifest is frozen and committed**; running the four candidates needs toolchains the repository does not carry.
- The last in-repo lever, the preregistered glossary-conditioned decoder pilot, ran the same day and was killed; see the addendum below.

No `bash autoresearch.sh` invocation occurred; the run budget is untouched.
