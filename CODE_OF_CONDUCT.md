# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Voiceour to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and gracefully accepting technical criticism. Disagreeing with a design is normal; the argument is about the design.
- Focusing on what is best for users of the software.

Behavior that does not:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at <vladtoie@gmail.com>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts for behavior they judge inappropriate, and will explain the decision when doing so is possible without exposing a reporter.
